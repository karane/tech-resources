# 90-Day Plan to Become Tech Lead

## Objective

Act as a Tech Lead before having the title. Focus on impact, team
performance, risk reduction, and strategic influence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 1 (Days 1--30): Become the Technical Reference

Goals:
- Deliver high-quality code consistently
- Write architectural documentation
- Identify hidden risks
- Provide structured code reviews

Outcome: Your name starts being mentioned in technical decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 2 (Days 31--60): Lead Without Title

Goals:
- Propose architectural improvements
- Organize technical standards
- Facilitate discussions
- Anticipate scaling risks

Mindset Shift: Think 6 months ahead in every technical decision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 3 (Days 61--90): Strategic Visibility

Goals:
- Send weekly structured technical summaries
- Connect technical decisions to business impact
- Reduce manager dependency
- Improve team predictability

Outcome: Promotion becomes a natural next step.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Immediate Action Plan (This Week)

1. Identify one invisible technical risk.
2. Actively unblock at least one teammate.
3. Send your first structured weekly technical summary.

Consistency builds perception. Perception builds leadership. Leadership
brings the title.
