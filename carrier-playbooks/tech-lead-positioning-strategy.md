# Tech Lead Positioning Strategy

## Do NOT Say:

"I want to be Tech Lead."

## Say Instead:

"I want to help improve team scalability and predictability."
"I've been thinking about structural improvements."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Tactical Actions

### 1. Ask Strategic Questions in Meetings

- Does this scale?
- What is the long-term risk?
- How does this impact cost?
- What happens in 6 months?

### 2. Solve Your Manager's Pain

Managers struggle with:
- Lack of predictability
- Production incidents
- Misalignment
- Technical conflicts

If you reduce these problems, you become indispensable.

### 3. Elevate, Don't Compete

Avoid:
- Public corrections
- Ego-driven discussions

Prefer:
- "Maybe we could consider..."
- "Another option might be..."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Common Promotion Blockers

- Focusing only on individual output
- Complaining about decisions
- Criticizing peers publicly
- Measuring yourself by volume instead of impact

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Signs You're Becoming the Informal Tech Lead

- "Ask [your name]"
- "What do you think?"
- "Can you lead this discussion?"

When this happens consistently, you are already acting as Tech Lead.
